# scan-non-english-special-treatments

Card scanning works well for ordinary English printings, but special treatments and non-English cards often mis-identify or rank poorly even when the scan fingerprint corpus already includes those printings. Players cannot trust collection or context scans against real cardboard when the wrong face or language wins.

Outcome: reliable identification (or a clear, usable fallback) for non-English printings and special-treatment variants so scan results match what the user is holding—not only English base arts.

Non-goals: redesigning camera UI; shipping a full collection manager; swapping to a wholly new vision stack without first measuring where the current fingerprint and match path fails for these cards.
