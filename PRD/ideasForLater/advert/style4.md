Hi everyone! For those of you that don't know me, my name is Chris, and I love Magic the Gathering. The last few months, I've been working on an app called TheJudge, and figured I'd share it here :)

You can ask it MTG questions, add cards to figure out how they interact, or lay out a stack to work through what's happening. It uses AI for the answers, and you can keep asking questions if something still doesn't make sense.

I've also put in a trade balancer and a life tracker with commander damage and counters. You can scan cards with your phone too.

If you have a look, let me know what you think. Feedback would be appreciated!
