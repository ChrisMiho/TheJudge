Hi everyone! For those of you that don't know me, my name is Chris, and I love Magic the Gathering. So much so that the last few months, I've been working on this app called TheJudge. Figured I'd share it here and show you all what I've been up to :)

I've been putting it together to help new players and old with MTG questions, stacks, trades, or just keeping track of a game. There's a few different things in it now.

You can ask a quick rules question or add cards to ask how they interact. If there's more going on, you can put in the players, life totals, turn phase, and relevant cards, including what's on the stack and in what order. The AI assistant uses that to help work through your question, and you can keep the conversation going if you need something explained a bit more. Answered questions are saved on the same device so you can go back to them later.

For trades, you can add cards to either side and compare their estimated USD values. It lets you choose the printing, foil or nonfoil, and how many copies, then adds it up for you. The price date is shown too. You can scan cards with your phone to add them to a trade or a question, or just search by name.

The life tracker works for 2–8 players and has commander damage, poison, energy, experience, custom counters, and day/night. It saves your game as you go. If a question comes up, you can bring the players, life totals, and counters over into a detailed question.

You can pick a Magic color theme or your own color too, and there's a feedback option in the menu if you run into something or have a suggestion.

That's where it's at so far. If you have a look, I'd love to hear what you think, or if there's anything you'd do differently!
