Hi everyone! For those of you that don't know me, my name is Chris, and I love Magic the Gathering. So much so that I've spent the last few months working on an app called TheJudge, and wanted to share it with you all.

It's meant to help new players and old with things that come up during a game. You can ask an MTG question, add a couple of cards to ask how they interact, or put in the game details and stack order for something more complicated. The answers come from an AI assistant, and you can ask follow-up questions too.

I've added a few other things alongside that. There's a trade balancer that compares the estimated values on both sides, with different printings and foils. You can search for cards or scan them with your phone.

There's also a life tracker with commander damage, poison, energy, experience, and custom counters. So you can just use it to keep track of a game if that's all you need.

Anyway, that's what I've been working on! If you check it out, I'd be happy to hear what you think so far :)
