# ui-reimagining-build

Problem: the owner settled direction 1 of the UI re-imagining across fourteen mockup rounds, but the shipped app still wears the old look and separate Quick and In-Depth doors. Outcome: the shipped app adopts the mana-colour ambience, the single Ask a Question door with the four-station In-depth flow, the two-pile Trade Balancer, the re-chromed card scan, and the shared menu sheets, matching the approved mockup. Non-goals: no change to the Life Tracker table itself, no work on directions 2 and 3, no new product questions beyond what the define gate raises. Intake is evidence only; every decision it proposes is made at the define gate.
