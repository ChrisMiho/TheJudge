# Idea: Flag Incorrect Ruling

Players who get a rule resolution from TheJudge have no fast way to say "this
is wrong" — the only path today is whatever general feedback flow exists, and
that's too much friction for a wrong ruling players notice mid-game. The
outcome is a single button on the answered rule-resolution screen that a
player taps once to flag that specific ruling as incorrect, capturing enough
of the interaction and context to investigate later without asking the player
to type anything.

Non-goals: no full feedback form or free-text explanation required to flag; no
real-time correction of the ruling; no reviewer/admin UI for triaging flags in
this pass. Whether this rides on the existing `sections/user-feedback/`
feature or needs its own path is a refinement question, not decided here.
