# Idea: scanner caution triangle always visible

In the Trade Balancer card scanner, the "experimental" caution triangle appears only after a card has been scanned, so players whose scans fail never see the warning. The outcome is that the triangle sits in the scanner's top-right from the moment the scanner opens (as in the direction-1 mockup), and the warning text appears only when the player taps the triangle. Non-goals: changing scan accuracy or the warning wording, touching other scanner surfaces, and the unrelated ui-pass-2 feedback items (1-4) that share the intake file.
