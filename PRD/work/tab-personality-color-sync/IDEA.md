# Tab personality and color sync

Owner request (verbatim): "i noticed the tabs for the app are kinda plain and boring, can we bring some personality to the tab? can we have it sync up with its respective color profile even?"

Problem: the app's tabs look plain; a player sees no character in them.
Outcome: tabs carry visible personality, and each tab's look follows its respective color profile (the MTG color identity theming the app already has).
Non-goals: no product truth decided here; what "personality" means, which tabs, and how "its respective color profile" maps are left to refinement.
