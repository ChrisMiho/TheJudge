## Question (as reshaped with the owner)

The owner's answers on 2026-10-10 changed the question in two ways.

1. **New idea — ask Luna what she understands.** The owner's other ideas are
   about organizing information for a human, and he is unsure they carry over to
   an agent. Instead: send Luna her real prompt with a precursor asking her to
   say what her task is and what is unclear, and use that to find what to tune.
2. **"100%" redefined — re-check when told it's wrong.** The owner's target is
   not a perfect first answer. It is: when a player says the answer is wrong,
   Luna re-evaluates and lands on the right answer.

Challenge raised: "re-evaluate" must not mean "agree with whoever pushes." At a
table, the player saying "that's wrong" is often the one losing the ruling. The
behavior to measure is: wrong answers get fixed on pushback, right answers hold
(with the rule cited), and right answers never flip.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-10)

- **Five full passes of the suite**, today's prompt, to measure how much answers
  vary. Cost is not a concern. Passes run side by side from one clean worktree,
  each its own run-id, each `--max-cost-usd` 15 (resume if a pass hits the cap).
- **Seeded split at analysis time:** a tuning half we study and a check half kept
  sealed until a candidate is final. Both halves are answered in every pass.
- **A miss is a disagreement with RulesGuru, not an error,** until checked
  against the Comprehensive Rules. Misses in all five passes are checked first.
- **Task-breakdown probe (owner's idea):** before answering, Luna describes the
  tasks the question needs her to do. Run on misses *and* on hits as a control.
  Owner's theory: if Luna always lists the right set of tasks, a correct ruling
  follows. Testable: score each breakdown right/wrong against the checked answer
  and see whether wrong breakdowns line up with wrong rulings.
- **Pushback test stays:** a scripted "that's wrong, check again" after the
  first answer; score fixed / held / flipped (flipped must be zero).
